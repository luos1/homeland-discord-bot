## 🚀 Release Notes Draft — Onboarding + Daily Rewards Monetization (2026-03-01)

### What shipped
- **New `/onboarding` quest flow (3-step):** character creation, first battle, and first gift via trade.
- **Daily attendance reward rework:** stronger milestones at day 3/7/30 (rare gear, legendary pet, premium days).
- **Invite rewards upgrade:** inviter now gets gems + gold + rare gear on successful referral onboarding.
- **New `/server-rewards` command:** claim one-time server invite rewards per guild (legendary gear + gems + premium days).
- **Gift-tracking integration completed:** successful trade now marks onboarding `gift_sent` progression to unlock premium trial reward.

### Revenue/retention intent
- Faster early progression reduces Day-1/Day-7 churn and improves referral conversion.
- Time-limited premium rewards (3/7/30-day hooks) create a stronger upsell bridge to paid premium.
- Server-level invite incentives increase bot distribution and top-of-funnel user inflow.
